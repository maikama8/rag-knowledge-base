# Northwind Field Guide

## Refunds

The refund window is 30 days from the purchase date. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

## Support

Support hours are weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00 WAT. Weekend coverage is limited to severity-one incidents.

## Travel

Rail is the default for staff travel to Abuja. Flights need a manager's approval when the fare is above 150000 NGN.
